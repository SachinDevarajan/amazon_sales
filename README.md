# 🛒 Amazon Sales Dashboard

# 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Amazon Sales Dashboard** developed **Power BI** for data cleaning, preprocessing, and analysis, and dashboard visualization.

The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profit trends, category-wise distribution, order fulfillment status, and yearly sales growth. It enables businesses to monitor performance and identify opportunities for improving profitability and operational efficiency.

---

# ❓ Business Problem

E-commerce businesses process thousands of orders across multiple product categories, brands, and countries. Tracking sales performance, profit margins, and order fulfillment manually can be time-consuming and inefficient.

The objective of this project is to:

✔ Analyze sales and profit performance

✔ Monitor yearly sales trends

✔ Evaluate category-wise contributions

✔ Understand order fulfillment efficiency

✔ Generate business insights through interactive reporting

# 💡 Key Insights

| KPI                       | Value  |
| ------------------------- | ------ |
| Total Orders              | 100K   |
| Total Profit              | 7.58M  |
| Total Sales               | 98.60M |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | 986.01 |
| Profit Margin             | 7.69%  |

### 📊 Business Insights

* Total sales generated reached **98.60 Million**.
* Overall profit recorded was **7.58 Million**.
* Average Order Value (AOV) stood at **986.01**.
* Electronics and Toys & Games contributed the highest sales volume.
* Sports & Outdoors generated the lowest sales among categories.
* Sales peaked in **2023**, indicating strong business performance.
* A significant portion of orders successfully reached the delivered stage.
* Returned and cancelled orders represented a small percentage of total orders.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

✔ Focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories such as Electronics and Toys & Games.

✔ Improve sales strategies for lower-performing categories to increase revenue contribution.

✔ Monitor returned and cancelled orders to identify operational issues.

✔ Expand successful product lines in top-performing markets.

✔ Use yearly sales trends for forecasting and inventory planning.
